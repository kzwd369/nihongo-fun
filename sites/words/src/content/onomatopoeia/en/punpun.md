## The Sound of Huffy Anger

プンプン (punpun) captures the kind of anger that puffs out your cheeks and makes you cross your arms — visible, expressive, and often a little endearing. It's a **gijougo** (擬情語), a word for emotional states, and it sits in a very specific spot on the anger spectrum: upset enough to show it, but not so furious that it feels dangerous.

## プンプン vs. イライラ

While イライラ describes simmering, internal irritation that builds over time, プンプン is more outward and demonstrative. Someone who is プンプン wants you to *know* they're upset — they might pout, huff, or stomp around. In anime and manga, プンプン anger is often drawn with steam coming out of a character's head or puffed-up cheeks. It tends to feel lighter and sometimes even cute compared to the sharper edge of イライラ.

## Fun Fact

プンプン also means a strong smell wafting through the air — 「タバコの匂いがプンプンする」 (tabako no nioi ga punpun suru, "it reeks of cigarettes"). The shared thread seems to be something radiating outward in an obvious, impossible-to-ignore way — whether that's the smell filling a room or someone's anger filling the atmosphere.
