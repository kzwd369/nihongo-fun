## The Sound of a Good Time

ワイワイ (waiwai) is the sound of people being cheerfully noisy together. It's a **giseigo** (擬声語) — a word that imitates actual sound — and it captures the buzzing, overlapping chatter of a group having fun. Think of an izakaya on Friday night, a school festival, or a family gathering where everyone's talking at once.

## Where You'll Hear ワイワイ

「ワイワイ騒ぐ」 (waiwai sawagu, "to make merry noise") and 「ワイワイ楽しむ」 (waiwai tanoshimu, "to have a lively good time") are the most common patterns. The word almost always carries a positive feeling — it's the noise of people enjoying themselves, not an annoying racket. If the noise is unsettling rather than fun, ザワザワ (zawazawa) might be a better fit. ワイワイ shows up often in subtitles and manga to set the scene of a cheerful, crowded space.

## Fun Fact

ワイワイ is sometimes written as わいわい in hiragana to give it a softer, more casual feel. In modern Japanese social media, ワイワイ has become a shorthand for "let's hang out and have fun" — you might see it in event invitations or group chat messages as a way to set the tone for a relaxed, lively gathering.
